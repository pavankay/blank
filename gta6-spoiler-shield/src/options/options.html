Packs

A pack is a JSON file with the names, places and spoiler words for one game or show. GTA VI comes built in. Packs installed from a link update themselves every 6 hours.

Categories

Tick what you want hidden. "Edit words" shows exactly what each category looks for. Your changes are saved separately and survive pack updates. Bold words are the ones Relaxed mode uses.

Detection

Extra checks for spoilers that don't use any obvious words.

Reports

Right-click a post and choose Report as spoiler to hide it, and any copies of it. If you add a server below, your reports are shared, and things enough other people report get hidden for you too.
