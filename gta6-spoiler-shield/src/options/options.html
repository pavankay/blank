Topic packs

Everything the shield knows about a game or show (names, places, spoiler words, categories) lives in a pack, not in the extension's code. Packs with an update link refresh themselves every few hours, so new spoiler words arrive without an extension update.

Categories

Each pack's categories, with the exact words they listen for. Your edits are kept separately, so they survive pack updates. Words marked ★ are "clear spoiler" words used by Relaxed mode.
